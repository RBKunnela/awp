# Glossary — PayBotFin doctrine

- **PayBotFin** — product / commercial brand: neutral hosted witness + operator tools for AI-agent proof.
- **AWP (Agent Witness Protocol)** — open protocol + free offline verifier; not the paid product.
- **Receipt (recebimento)** — portable evidence file (`receipt.json` / pack) for a consequential agent act; issued by a witness/product, verified by AWP.
- **Issuer** — who produces the receipt body (PayBotFin in our product plane).
- **Optional product** — PayBotFin is not required to use open AWP; required only for product issuance of real receipts.
- **Pro list** — €11 per bot per month + 1.5% volume commission (public list; + Finnish ALV note on site).
- **Enterprise** — custom commission / contract terms.
- **Neutral witness** — independent party producing tamper-evident records; not “sign your own log only.”
